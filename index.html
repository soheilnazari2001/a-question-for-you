Elahe, I am sorry.

Dear Elahe,

Thank you for being there for me, no matter what. Even when things were hard, even when I gave you no reason to stay, you stayed. I don't think I ever told you how much that meant to me. I am telling you now, from the bottom of my heart.

I am sorry for the many times I made you cry. I am sorry for my words when they were sharp, and for my actions when they were careless. You deserved kindness from me, and too many times I gave you pain instead. I see that clearly now.

I am sorry for making your life feel like hell. Your days should have felt light and safe with me, and I made them heavy. That is not something I can simply take back, but I can tell you honestly that I am sorry.

I am also sorry for the harm I caused beyond us. I am sorry for the damage to the two relationships that mattered to you, and for breaking the one we had together. I am sorry for every part I played, and I will not make excuses for any of it.

I never wanted any of this to happen. I did not know what I was doing, and I am not saying that to excuse myself. I am saying it because it is the truth, and you deserve the truth. You are the most valuable person in the world, and I treated you as if you were not. That was my mistake, never yours.

You do not owe me anything. Not forgiveness, not friendship, not an answer that makes me feel better. Whatever you feel is allowed, and whatever you decide is okay. I just wanted you to know how I see it now, and how truly sorry I am.

At the end of this letter are two questions. Please answer them honestly. There is no wrong answer, and nothing you choose will make me respect you less. Take all the time you need.

With honesty and respect, — Me
